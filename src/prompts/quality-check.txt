Then these checks for ALL kinds:
1. Clarity: it is clear what is being said and why it matters.

3. Human voice: it must read like a real person with a take, not a detached news anchor. FAIL posts built from canned significance phrasing ("underscores the", "a reminder of how", "the stakes are high") or generic advice closers ("stay informed", "ensure your defenses are strong", "make sure your systems are updated", "you could be next").
4. Safety: nothing that would help someone run a scam or carry out an attack; no individualized professional (legal/medical/financial) advice.
5. Format: 280 characters or fewer (a link, if present, counts as 23), at most two hashtags.
